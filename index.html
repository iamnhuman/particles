GRAIN turns a single track into a living particle sculpture. Low frequencies push the form outward. Midrange bends its filaments. High frequencies scatter its light.

The music is examined in overlapping windows of 1,024 samples. Every attack leaves its own trace, all moving to the same audio clock.
